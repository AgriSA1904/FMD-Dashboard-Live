Data reconciliation

Minister vs. provincial JOC.

We compare figures from official ministerial briefings with data reported through provincial Joint Operations Committee engagements. Discrepancies are noted and tracked. Where gaps exist, we raise the concern for formal clarification.

Data reconciliation note A significant gap exists between nationally reported procurement figures and provincial receipt data.
A total of 6 000 000 doses have been reported as procured nationally. Provincial Joint Operations Committee returns account for 3 472 758 doses received at provincial level, a gap of 2 527 242 doses. A formal reconciliation of these figures has not yet been made available. We raise this concern in the interest of transparency and accurate programme reporting.
